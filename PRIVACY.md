# Privacy

These apps collect nothing and send nothing. They have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and
no network access. Your photos, files and notes stay on your phone, in files you can read yourself.

Permissions, and what each is for:

- Gallery: read photos and videos; all files access (to move, hide and trash media); show over other
  apps and notifications (the floating video player); fingerprint or screen lock (the hidden album).
- Files: all files access (to browse and manage storage); install packages (to open APKs you tap).
- Notes: none.
- Home: lists the apps installed on the phone so it can show and start them.

None of the apps can reach the internet.

If this ever changes it will be written here first.
